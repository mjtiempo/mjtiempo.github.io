Clippy

Omarchy plugin · QML · MIT License

A clipboard history picker for the Omarchy bar. Click the icon in the bar and a popup shows your recent clipboard entries — text and images — then click one to copy it back.

  • Pure viewer: no watchers or background processes
  • Reads Omarchy's built-in clipboard history
  • Images copy as images, ready to paste anywhere
  • Keyboard friendly: Esc closes, hover highlights
